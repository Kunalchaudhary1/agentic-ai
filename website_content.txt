How do I search for a baby wishlist?
As soon as the baby wishlist is public, start with the "Find a baby wishlist" search bar at the top of the page. In advanced search, you can use the name, city or county of a baby wishlist owner to find the one you are looking for.
How do I create an Amazon baby wishlist?
Ready to get started? Let's do this. Start with the "Create a baby wishlist" button at the top of the page, sign in or create an Amazon account, and follow the on-screen instructions.
How do I qualify for Free Gift?
To receive your free gift, you just need to create a baby wishlist and wait 5 days before redeeming your free gift. Add at least INR 750 worth of qualifying items - baby products that are shipped and sold by Amazon - from your Amazon baby wishlist to your shopping basket.
How does the Amazon baby wishlist checklist work?
The Amazon baby wishlist checklist makes it easy to create your wishlist and keep track of what you still need to add. Explore the list of recommended items and check off any you already have or don't need. Add items you need to your wishlist from anywhere on amazon.in - we'll automatically check off items when you do.
Does Amazon provide inspiration for baby wishlist idea?
Amazon provides you with ideas and inspiration for every age and stage as well as shopping guides for mom.
